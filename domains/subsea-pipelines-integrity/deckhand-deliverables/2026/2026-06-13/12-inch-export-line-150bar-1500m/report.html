12-inch Export Line Wall Thickness Quick Screen

Summary

For a 12-inch export line at 150 bar and 1500 m water depth: with buckle arrestors, selected standard wall is SCH 80 / 17.475 mm. Without buckle arrestors, selected standard wall is SCH 140 / 28.575 mm. Buckle arrestor basis is design data, not a guess.

Objective

Screen required wall thickness for the stated export-line pressure and water depth, using the approved wall-thickness quick-check calculation.

Inputs & Assumptions

  • OD: 323.9 mm, treated as NPS 12 / 12.75 in OD.
  • Design pressure: 150 bar; external hydrostatic pressure: 15.083 MPa at 1500 m.
  • Assumed X65 line pipe, SMYS 448 MPa, SMTS 531 MPa.
  • Assumed corrosion allowance: 3 mm.
  • Buckle-arrestor basis: unresolved by user, so both branches are shown.

Methodology

The approved quick-check sweep evaluates ASME B36.10 12-inch standard walls and a 10–35 mm wall grid. Burst/containment is checked at design internal pressure with zero external pressure; collapse and propagation are checked for the empty line at full water depth.

Two-Branch Comparison

The with-arrestors branch excludes propagation buckling from headline wall selection but reports it separately. The without-arrestors branch includes propagation buckling as a design requirement.

Results

Selected/default branch for screening: with buckle arrestors. Alternate branch: without buckle arrestors / design for propagation buckling.

Integral arrestor screen: pipe wall 17.475 mm, arrestor wall 25.500 mm, additional wall 8.025 mm, length 0.648 m (2.0D screening length for integral arrestor section).

Code Checks

Codes checked: DNV-ST-F101 and API RP 1111. With arrestors, governing check is DNV-ST-F101 collapse at utilisation 0.629. Without arrestors, governing check is DNV-ST-F101 propagation_buckling at utilisation 0.719.

Assumptions & Sensitivity

The branch decision changes selected standard wall from 17.475 mm to 28.575 mm, an increase of 11.100 mm. Material grade, corrosion allowance, safety class, installation bending, fatigue, and local code factors can move the final design wall.

Conclusions

Do not quote a single nominal wall until the buckle-arrestor / propagation-buckling basis is confirmed. For early screening, carry both SCH 80 and SCH 140 branches.

Limitations

This is a quick screen, not an issued-for-design wall selection. It excludes installation bending, spanning/VIV, fatigue, thermal expansion, route-specific pressure profiles, mill tolerance, ovality, and project safety-class calibration.
