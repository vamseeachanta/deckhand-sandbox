12-inch Export Line Wall Thickness Quick Screen

Buckle arrestor basis is unresolved; both branches are reported.

summary

Quick-screen result: with buckle arrestors, smallest passing standard wall is 17.475 mm (SCH 80). Without buckle arrestors, design for propagation buckling gives 28.575 mm (SCH 140).

Design-basis caveat: buckle arrestor basis is not supplied, so both branches are reported; do not treat one branch as final design.

objective

Screen wall thickness for a 12-inch export line at 150 bar internal design pressure and 1500 m water depth.

inputs_assumptions

  • OD 323.9 mm, X65, SMYS 448 MPa, SMTS 531 MPa.
  • Corrosion allowance 3 mm.
  • External pressure 15.083 MPa from seawater at 1500 m.
  • Codes checked: DNV-ST-F101 and API RP 1111.
  • Buckle arrestors unresolved; both branches shown.

two_branch_comparison

  • With buckle arrestors: 17.475 mm (SCH 80); non-standard minimum 14.909 mm; governing DNV-ST-F101 collapse, utilisation 0.629.
  • Without buckle arrestors: 28.575 mm (SCH 140); non-standard minimum 25.408 mm; governing DNV-ST-F101 propagation_buckling, utilisation 0.719.

methodology

Approved deterministic quick check sweeps wall thickness and selects the smallest ASME B36.10 standard wall with all applicable utilisation ratios ≤ 1.0. burst/containment checked at p_i = design pressure with zero external pressure (route minimum); collapse and propagation checked for the empty line (p_i = 0) at full water depth.

results

  • Selected/default branch recorded: with buckle arrestors.
  • Alternate branch recorded: without buckle arrestors / propagation buckling included.
  • Integral buckle arrestor screening: pipe wall 17.475 mm, arrestor wall 25.500 mm over 0.648 m (2.0D screening length for integral arrestor section).

code_checks

DNV-ST-F101 and API RP 1111 quick-screen checks were run. DNV collapse governs the with-arrestor branch; DNV propagation buckling governs the no-arrestor branch.

Assumptions & Sensitivity

The wall changes strongly with the buckle-arrestor decision: SCH 80 with arrestors versus SCH 140 without arrestors. Material grade, corrosion allowance, ovality, installation strain, mill tolerance, and safety-class factors can move the final purchase wall.

conclusions

For this quick screen, use 17.5 mm only if buckle arrestors are part of the design basis. If not, use about 28.6 mm for propagation buckling screening before detailed design checks.

limitations

Quick screen only. It excludes installation bending/strain, fatigue, free spans, on-bottom stability, thermal expansion, route-specific low/high points, local regulation, fabrication tolerance, and project design factors not supplied.
